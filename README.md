# cricket_pisso_templates

Match Card Studio: a single-page tool for making Cricket Pisso social posts.

Open `index.html` in a browser, pick a post type, upload a photo, fill in the details and download a PNG.

## Post types

**Match updates:** Match day (one main photo, or two side-by-side photos or flags), Toss result, Playing XI (captain and wicket-keeper menus), Wicket alert, Innings break (target, run rate and a chase poll with emojis), Last match result, Injury update

**Stats and analysis:** Player figures, Player analysis, Key player battle (two player photos), Team stats battle, Ground analysis, Pitch report, Weather report

**Fan posts:** Captain's call (bat or bowl poll), Toss prediction, Playing XI prediction, Meme

## Features

- Square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350) and story (1080 × 1920) sizes
- Drag to position the photo, both logos and the page name
- Remove the plain background from a logo
- Team colour picker
- Frames: 9 designs, one colour or a blend of two or three, with ready-made colour mixes
- Text, logos and positions are remembered in the browser
